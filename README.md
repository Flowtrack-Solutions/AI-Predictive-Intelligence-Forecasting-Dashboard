# AI Predictive Intelligence & Forecasting Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates an AI-powered executive business intelligence dashboard developed using Power BI.

The dashboard combines financial analytics, profitability analysis, predictive forecasting, and AI-driven strategic recommendations to support executive-level decision-making.

The objective of this project was not only to visualize business data, but also to create a dynamic decision-support solution capable of generating actionable insights from historical performance trends.

---

## Key Features

- Executive KPI Monitoring
- Revenue & Profitability Analysis
- AI Strategic Recommendations
- Forecast Revenue & Profit Projection
- Dynamic Risk Assessment
- Interactive Date Filtering
- DAX-Based Business Logic
- Forecast Trend Visualization
- Executive-Level Dashboard Design

---

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Financial Overview
Provides a high-level overview of:
- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Revenue Growth
- Product Profitability
- Country-wise Revenue Performance

Includes dynamic AI Executive Summary based on selected date range.

---

### 2. Revenue & Profitability Analysis
Focuses on:
- Profitability trends
- Discount impact analysis
- Most profitable products
- Least profitable products
- Segment-wise profitability
- Revenue vs Cost Analysis

Includes AI-generated profitability insights for business decision support.

---

### 3. AI Predictive Intelligence & Forecasting
Provides:
- Forecasted Revenue & Profit
- Revenue Trend Signals
- Business Risk Assessment
- Revenue Forecast Outlook
- Profit Forecast & Margin Outlook
- AI Strategic Recommendations

Forecasting logic is dynamically driven using historical performance patterns and trend analysis.
